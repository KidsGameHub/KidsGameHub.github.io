🔒 Privacy Policy

KidsGameHub offers free games for children. We keep things simple: we do not want any personal information from kids or parents.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, sign-ups, chats or comments, and we do not ask for names, emails or any other personal details.

Ads, cookies and tracking

This site does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking cookies. Game scores stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) when pages are visited. We do not receive or use this information. See GitHub's own privacy statement for details.

Children

KidsGameHub is made for children and their families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we ever add features that change this policy, we will update this page first.
